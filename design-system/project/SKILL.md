Key reminders for SeoulMate:
- Korean UI, 해요체 (warm polite-formal). No emoji decoration.
- Dark always. Page `#0D0D0D`, surfaces `#1A1A1A`.
- Color accents: Morning Amber `#F5A623` + Blush Pink `#E8547A` only. No coral/purple gradient.
- Pretendard Variable for type. Numbers in JetBrains Mono with tabular figures.
- Real Seoul night photography over hand-drawn illustration.
- Mobile-first; max content 430px; bottom nav with center FAB.
- Lucide-style stroke icons.
